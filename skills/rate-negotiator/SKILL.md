## 1. When to use it

Modeling compensation scenarios, analyzing recruiter compensation bands, or drafting counter-offer scripts for senior engineering and consulting mandates.


## 2. Required inputs and access

- Job offer, recruiter compensation range, or mandate RFP
- Market benchmark data ($115k-$140k+ CAD FTE, $95-$125/hr B2B contract)

## 3. Sequence of work

1. Determine employment structure: full-time permanent (FTE) vs independent B2B contract.
2. Calculate total compensation equivalency (base salary, bonus, healthcare, pension, vacation).
3. Establish aspirational anchor, target outcome, and strict reservation floor.
4. Draft phone scripts and email counter-offers adopting 'Warm tone, firm numbers'.
5. Prepare deflection techniques for premature salary expectation queries.

## 4. How to validate the result

All compensation figures grounded in verified market ranges; reservation floor never disclosed prematurely.


## 5. What to return

Compensation comparison model, counter-offer scenario matrix, and verbatim negotiation scripts.


## 6. What requires approval

Never send compensation counters directly to recruiters; operator conducts all live communications.
