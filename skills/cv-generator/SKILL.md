> Save the process we just used as a skill called “CV Generator”.

A useful skill states the six fields from [Skills and routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations).

## 1. When to use it

Creating or refactoring executive, senior engineering, or consulting resumes into ATS-compliant single-column layouts within a strict 2-page budget.


## 2. Required inputs and access

- Candidate profile, history, and target role specification
- Design constraints (single-column, high-contrast typography, KPI callout cards)

## 3. Sequence of work

1. Draft executive summary highlighting core engineering differentiator and years of experience.
2. Construct 3-pillar KPI callout card section highlighting verified quantitative wins.
3. Structure chronological work history with quantifiable bullet points (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
4. Include technical competency inventory categorized by domain (Endpoint, Cloud, Scripting, DevOps).
5. Format strictly for 2.0 pages, eliminating orphan lines and page-3 spillovers.

## 4. How to validate the result

Strict 2-page length budget respected. Single-column body layout. No buzzwords.


## 5. What to return

Complete ATS-optimized markdown CV draft ready for PDF compilation or word processor import.


## 6. What requires approval

Never submit CV directly to recruiters or job boards without candidate sign-off.
